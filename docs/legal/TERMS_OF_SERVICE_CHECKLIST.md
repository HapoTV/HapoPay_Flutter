# Terms of Service checklist (not legal advice)

Draft and host public HTTPS Terms of Service before store submission.
Counsel should review. Settings → Terms stay inert until a real URL exists.

## Must include

- **Agreement** — Using HapoPay means accepting these Terms; age / capacity to contract; parents accepting for minors where required.
- **Service description** — Student spending wallet, parent controls (limits, lock, ledger), rewards, QR payments via the HapoPay backend — not a bank; not FDIC/similar insurance unless you actually provide it.
- **Accounts** — Accurate registration, credential security, one account per user, parent–student linking rules, prohibition on sharing accounts.
- **Acceptable use** — No fraud, reverse engineering of payment flows, scraping, abuse of QR pay, or circumventing spending limits / locks.
- **Payments & balances** — How top-ups and campus payments work; that the Django ledger is authoritative; dispute / “charged twice” support path; no guarantee of merchant availability.
- **Rewards** — Points/achievements are promotional; can change; no cash value unless stated.
- **Intellectual property** — App, branding, and content ownership; limited license to use the client.
- **Third parties** — Campus merchants, cloud providers, crash reporting (Sentry); their terms may apply.
- **Disclaimers** — Service “as available”; no warranty of uninterrupted campus connectivity.
- **Limitation of liability** — Caps and exclusions appropriate to your jurisdiction and product.
- **Indemnity** — User liability for misuse.
- **Suspension / termination** — When accounts can be locked (fraud, ToS breach); effect on balances (owner policy).
- **Governing law & disputes** — Jurisdiction, arbitration/courts, class-action waiver if used.
- **Changes** — How Terms updates are published and when they take effect.
- **Contact** — Support email (align with privacy contact where sensible).

## Hosting (owner)

1. Publish at a stable HTTPS URL (e.g. `https://hapopay.com/terms`).
2. Link from Settings, registration disclaimer, and store listings.
3. Keep Privacy Policy and Terms versioned with effective dates.

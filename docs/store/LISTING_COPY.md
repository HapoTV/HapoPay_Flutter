## Full description

```
HapoPay helps students spend on campus while parents stay in control.

FOR STUDENTS
• See your balance and recent activity in one place
• Pay with QR — fast checkout at participating campus spots
• Unlock rewards as you spend smart — tiers, streaks, and achievements

FOR PARENTS
• Set spending limits that match your family’s rules
• Lock or unlock a student’s card when you need to
• Follow the family ledger and live activity feed

Built for real campus life: secure sign-in, optional biometric unlock, and a clear view of where the money goes.

HapoPay is a spending companion for school communities — not a bank. Campus availability depends on your school’s merchants and your family’s setup.
```
